# FMG → Indian Medical Registration Pathway

This page provides a high-level overview of the pathway for a Foreign Medical Graduate (FMG) seeking medical registration in India.

> **Important:** Requirements and procedures can change. Always verify the current requirements with the relevant official authority before taking action.

## Overview

The broad pathway may involve:

1. Completing a qualifying medical degree outside India
2. Meeting the applicable eligibility requirements
3. Appearing for the Foreign Medical Graduate Examination (FMGE), where applicable
4. Passing the FMGE
5. Obtaining the required provisional registration
6. Completing the required internship / supervised training in India
7. Completing any required documentation and verification
8. Obtaining permanent medical registration
9. Proceeding to medical employment, postgraduate education, or other career pathways

## 1. Medical Degree

The first step is completion of a medical qualification from an institution outside India.

The exact requirements applicable to a particular graduate depend on factors including:

- Country of medical education
- Date of admission
- Date of graduation
- Recognition of the medical qualification
- Applicable NMC regulations
- Individual circumstances

## 2. FMGE

FMGE is the screening examination historically used for eligible foreign medical graduates seeking registration to practise medicine in India.

The examination is conducted by the National Board of Examinations in Medical Sciences (NBEMS).

Always check the latest official notification for:

- Eligibility
- Application dates
- Required documents
- Examination format
- Fees
- Result requirements

## 3. After Passing FMGE

Passing FMGE does not by itself mean that an FMG can immediately practise independently as a registered medical practitioner.

The graduate may still need to complete the applicable registration and internship requirements.

The exact sequence depends on the regulations and instructions applicable at the time.

## 4. Provisional Registration

Eligible FMGs may need provisional registration with the appropriate authority before undertaking the required internship or supervised clinical training.

The applicable authority and documentation requirements should be verified from current official sources.

## 5. Internship / Supervised Training

Where required, an FMG completes the applicable period of internship or supervised clinical training in India.

Important considerations may include:

- Eligibility of the institution
- Duration of training
- State-specific requirements
- NMC requirements
- Provisional registration
- Documentation
- Completion certificate

## 6. Permanent Registration

After completing the applicable requirements, the graduate may apply for permanent medical registration with the relevant authority.

The exact process depends on the applicable regulations and the State Medical Council / competent authority involved.

## 7. After Registration

Once appropriately registered, an FMG may consider several career pathways, depending on eligibility:

### Clinical employment

- Government hospitals
- Private hospitals
- Primary care
- Other clinical positions

### Postgraduate education

- NEET-PG / applicable Indian postgraduate pathways
- Other recognised postgraduate qualifications

### International pathways

Depending on eligibility and the destination country:

- UK
- Ireland
- Australia
- Middle East
- Other jurisdictions

Each international pathway has separate registration and licensing requirements.

## Important distinction

Throughout this guide, we should distinguish between:

**Official requirements**

Information published by NMC, NBEMS, State Medical Councils, government authorities, universities, or other competent bodies.

**Institution-specific requirements**

Rules or procedures imposed by a particular hospital, medical college, university, or State Medical Council.

**Community experience**

Experiences reported by individual FMGs.

**Interpretation / advice**

Explanations or practical suggestions that are not themselves official requirements.

Keeping these categories separate is one of the core principles of this project.
