# FMGE Application Process

**Last verified:** 23 September 2026

This page explains the general process for applying for the Foreign Medical Graduate Examination (FMGE).

> **Important:** Application dates, fees, document requirements, correction windows and other procedures can change between examination sessions. The current NBEMS Information Bulletin for the relevant session is the controlling document.

---

# 1. Who Conducts FMGE?

The Foreign Medical Graduate Examination (FMGE) is conducted by the **National Board of Examinations in Medical Sciences (NBEMS)**.

NBEMS publishes a separate Information Bulletin for each examination session.

The current FMGE examination page should be checked before every application.

---

# 2. FMGE Is Conducted in Sessions

FMGE is conducted in separate examination sessions.

The NBEMS website currently lists:

- June session
- December / later-year session

The exact dates and application window for each session are published by NBEMS.

Do not rely on dates from previous sessions.

---

# 3. Before Starting the Application

Prepare the documents and information required by the current Information Bulletin.

This may include:

### Identity information

- Passport
- Government identification, where applicable
- Citizenship / OCI documentation
- Personal details

### Medical qualification

- Primary medical qualification
- Degree / provisional degree documentation
- Academic records
- Supporting documents required by NBEMS

### Personal information

- Date of birth
- Father's / mother's details where required
- Permanent and correspondence address
- Email address
- Mobile number

### Examination information

- Previous FMGE attempts, if applicable
- Previous application details
- Other information requested in the current application form

The exact document requirements should always be checked against the current bulletin.

---

# 4. Online Application

FMGE applications are submitted through the online NBEMS examination system.

The general process is:

```text
Read current Information Bulletin
              ↓
Create / access candidate account
              ↓
Fill application form
              ↓
Upload required documents
              ↓
Upload photograph / signature / other required images
              ↓
Select examination city
              ↓
Pay examination fee
              ↓
Submit application
              ↓
Download / retain application documents
```

The exact sequence and available options may change between sessions.

---

# 5. Read the Information Bulletin First

Before filling the application, read the complete Information Bulletin for the relevant session.

Pay particular attention to:

- Eligibility
- Important dates
- Application procedure
- Fee
- Required documents
- Photograph requirements
- Signature requirements
- Image upload requirements
- Examination city selection
- Correction window
- Admit card
- Test-day requirements
- Deficient-document requirements
- Result and pass-certificate procedures

A candidate should not treat an older bulletin as the current rule.

---

# 6. Registration and Application Form

The application form should be completed carefully.

Enter information exactly as supported by the relevant official documents.

Particular attention should be paid to:

- Name
- Date of birth
- Passport details
- Citizenship
- Medical qualification
- University / institution details
- Dates of study
- Contact information

Avoid unnecessary abbreviations or variations in names.

If the application information does not match supporting documents, the candidate may face verification or eligibility problems.

---

# 7. Photograph and Image Requirements

NBEMS specifies technical requirements for photographs, signatures and other uploaded images.

These requirements may include:

- File format
- File size
- Image dimensions
- Background
- Recent photograph requirements
- Signature format
- Other image specifications

These requirements can change.

Always use the **image-upload instructions published for the relevant FMGE session** rather than instructions from an older examination.

---

# 8. Examination City

Candidates may be required to select an examination city from the options available during the application process.

Important:

> Selection of a city does not necessarily mean that the candidate will receive that exact examination centre.

The final examination centre is communicated through the admit card / official NBEMS process.

Candidates should therefore check the final admit card carefully.

---

# 9. Examination Fee

The examination fee is specified in the current FMGE Information Bulletin.

Because examination fees can change, this guide does not hard-code a fee amount.

Before payment:

- Verify the current fee
- Verify the payment deadline
- Confirm that the payment was successful
- Save the transaction details
- Retain the payment receipt / confirmation

Do not rely on fee amounts quoted in old Telegram, WhatsApp or social-media posts.

---

# 10. Final Submission

Before submitting the application, check the complete form carefully.

Recommended checklist:

- [ ] Name matches official documents
- [ ] Date of birth is correct
- [ ] Passport details are correct
- [ ] Citizenship / OCI information is correct
- [ ] Medical qualification details are correct
- [ ] University / institution details are correct
- [ ] Uploaded documents are readable
- [ ] Photograph satisfies current requirements
- [ ] Signature satisfies current requirements
- [ ] Examination city preference is correct
- [ ] Contact details are correct
- [ ] Fee payment has been completed
- [ ] Final application has been successfully submitted

Keep copies of the submitted application and payment confirmation.

---

# 11. Application Number and Records

After successful submission, retain the information generated by the application system.

Keep copies of:

- Application form
- Application number
- Payment receipt
- Uploaded documents
- Confirmation page
- Important emails
- SMS notifications

These records can be useful if a problem arises later.

---

# 12. Correction Window

NBEMS may provide a correction / edit window after the application submission period.

The scope of permissible corrections is determined by the relevant examination bulletin and notices.

Not every field necessarily remains editable.

Therefore:

> Do not intentionally enter incorrect information assuming that it can always be corrected later.

Candidates should check the official correction-window notice for the specific session.

---

# 13. Deficient Documents

NBEMS may identify deficiencies or require additional documentation from an applicant.

Candidates should regularly check:

- NBEMS website
- Candidate login
- Registered email address
- Registered mobile number
- Official notices

If NBEMS provides a deadline for submitting deficient documents, the candidate should respond within that deadline.

The June 2026 FMGE cycle, for example, included an NBEMS notice concerning a final opportunity to submit deficient documents. citeturn0search1

---

# 14. Admit Card

The admit card is released online through NBEMS.

Candidates should download it from the official website / candidate portal.

For the June 2026 session, NBEMS specified that admit cards would be made available on its website and would **not** be sent by post or email. citeturn0search4

After downloading the admit card, check:

- Candidate name
- Photograph
- Roll number
- Examination date
- Examination centre
- Reporting time
- Test-day instructions

Any discrepancy should be addressed through the official NBEMS process.

---

# 15. Test-Day Documents

The exact documents required on examination day are specified in the current Information Bulletin.

Candidates should read the test-day instructions carefully and carry the required documents in the required format.

Do not assume that documents accepted in an older FMGE session will automatically be accepted in a later session.

---

# 16. After the Examination

After the examination:

```text
FMGE examination
       ↓
Result
       ↓
Pass / fail status
       ↓
Pass-certificate process where applicable
       ↓
Provisional registration / CRMI pathway
       ↓
Completion of required internship
       ↓
Permanent registration pathway
```

Passing FMGE does **not** by itself complete the medical-registration process.

The subsequent requirements depend on the regulations and registration pathway applicable to the individual.

---

# 17. Common Application Mistakes

### Mistake 1 — Using an old bulletin

Requirements can change between sessions.

### Mistake 2 — Incorrect name

Use the name supported by the relevant official documents.

### Mistake 3 — Poor-quality uploads

Unreadable or technically non-compliant documents can cause problems.

### Mistake 4 — Missing the correction window

If a correction window is provided, check which fields can actually be edited and the deadline.

### Mistake 5 — Ignoring NBEMS notices

Important deficiencies or additional-document requests may be communicated through official notices.

### Mistake 6 — Losing payment records

Always retain the transaction details and application confirmation.

### Mistake 7 — Assuming application equals eligibility

Submission of an application does not necessarily mean that NBEMS has finally determined the candidate to be eligible.

---

# 18. Recommended Application Workflow

A practical workflow is:

### Step 1
Find the current FMGE session page on NBEMS.

### Step 2
Download the current Information Bulletin.

### Step 3
Read eligibility and document requirements.

### Step 4
Prepare documents before opening the application form.

### Step 5
Complete the application carefully.

### Step 6
Review every field before final submission.

### Step 7
Complete the fee payment.

### Step 8
Save the final application and payment records.

### Step 9
Monitor NBEMS notices and candidate login.

### Step 10
Complete any correction or deficient-document process within the specified deadline.

### Step 11
Download the admit card when released.

### Step 12
Follow the current test-day instructions.
