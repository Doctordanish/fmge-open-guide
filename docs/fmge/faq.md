# FMGE Frequently Asked Questions

**Last verified:** 23 September 2026

This FAQ answers common questions about FMGE, medical registration, CRMI and the post-FMGE pathway for Foreign Medical Graduates (FMGs).

> **Important:** FMGE, NMC and State Medical Council procedures can change. Where a question depends on a specific examination session, admission cohort, state or institution, verify the current official instructions.

---

# 1. What is FMGE?

The **Foreign Medical Graduate Examination (FMGE)** is the screening examination conducted by the National Board of Examinations in Medical Sciences (NBEMS) for eligible foreign medical graduates who require it as part of the pathway to medical registration in India.

It should not be confused with the postgraduate entrance examination or with medical registration itself.

---

# 2. Who needs to pass FMGE?

A foreign medical graduate may be required to qualify through FMGE before obtaining medical registration in India, subject to the regulations applicable to that individual.

Eligibility can depend on factors including:

- Admission date
- Foreign medical qualification
- Citizenship / OCI status
- Applicable NMC regulations
- Transitional provisions
- Other regulatory requirements

See the [FMGE Eligibility](index.md) page for the general framework.

---

# 3. Does every foreign medical graduate need FMGE?

Not necessarily.

The applicable regulatory framework determines whether an individual is required to qualify through FMGE or falls within an applicable exemption or different pathway.

Do not determine this solely from:

- Country of study
- University name
- Advice from another FMG
- Agent / consultant statements

Check the applicable NMC regulations and current NBEMS instructions.

---

# 4. Is my foreign university "NMC approved"?

NMC does not provide a simple universal list that can be treated as an endorsement of every foreign medical university.

The relevant question is whether the **individual foreign medical qualification and course of study satisfy the applicable regulatory requirements**.

An applicant should therefore examine:

- Admission date
- Course structure
- Duration
- Clinical training
- Recognition in the country of qualification
- Registration / eligibility requirements
- Applicable NMC regulations

---

# 5. What is the FMGE passing mark?

The FMGE passing standard is:

**150 marks out of 300.**

This is a 50% qualifying standard.

FMGE is not passed by achieving a particular percentile.

---

# 6. Is there negative marking in FMGE?

No.

The FMGE examination scheme does not use negative marking.

Candidates should nevertheless follow the current examination instructions for the relevant session.

---

# 7. How many questions are there in FMGE?

There are **300 questions**.

They are divided into:

| Part | Questions | Time |
|---|---:|---:|
| Part A | 150 | 150 minutes |
| Part B | 150 | 150 minutes |
| Total | 300 | 300 minutes |

---

# 8. How many attempts are allowed?

There is no general attempt limit under the FMGE examination scheme.

A candidate who does not qualify may apply for a subsequent session, subject to meeting the requirements applicable to that session.

---

# 9. Can I apply for FMGE again if I failed?

Yes.

A candidate who does not qualify may apply for a subsequent FMGE session, provided the candidate continues to satisfy the applicable eligibility requirements.

A new application must be completed according to the instructions for the new session.

---

# 10. Does passing FMGE give me a medical licence?

**No.**

Passing FMGE is one part of the pathway.

Depending on the applicable regulations, further steps may include:

```text
FMGE pass
   ↓
Provisional registration
   ↓
CRMI / internship
   ↓
Completion documentation
   ↓
Permanent registration
```

The exact pathway depends on the candidate's circumstances and applicable regulations.

---

# 11. What is CRMI?

**Compulsory Rotating Medical Internship (CRMI)** is the supervised internship stage required under the applicable medical-registration framework.

For an FMG who is required to complete CRMI in India, it may occur after the relevant examination and provisional-registration stage.

See the [FMG Internship & CRMI](../internship/index.md) guide.

---

# 12. Do I need to complete CRMI after passing FMGE?

This depends on the applicable regulatory requirements and the candidate's individual circumstances.

An FMG should not assume that every candidate has exactly the same internship requirement.

Check:

- Applicable NMC regulations
- Provisional registration requirements
- State Medical Council instructions
- Current CRMI allocation procedures

---

# 13. Does passing FMGE automatically give me a CRMI seat?

**No.**

The existence of CRMI seats in an institution-level NMC matrix should not be interpreted as an automatic individual allotment.

State-level procedures and institutional requirements may apply.

For example, the NMC's 2026–27 CRMI allocation framework provides for State Medical Councils, in consultation with the relevant Directorate of Medical Education, to allot CRMI slots according to applicable criteria.

---

# 14. Can I choose any hospital for CRMI?

Not automatically.

The institution must satisfy the applicable regulatory and allocation requirements.

The actual process may depend on:

- NMC allocation
- State Medical Council
- Directorate of Medical Education
- Institutional eligibility
- State-specific allotment procedure

A hospital appearing on a list does not necessarily mean an FMG can independently walk in and claim a seat.

---

# 15. Can a private medical college offer CRMI to an FMG?

Potentially, where the institution has CRMI capacity allocated under the applicable framework and the candidate is eligible.

However, the presence of a private institution in a CRMI matrix does not itself establish an individual's entitlement to join.

Always verify the current allocation and state procedure.

---

# 16. Is an internship fee legal?

Do not assume that every fee is either automatically valid or automatically invalid.

Different institutions may have different charges, while regulatory requirements can also apply.

Before paying:

- Ask for the official fee structure
- Ask for written confirmation
- Request an official receipt
- Identify what the payment is for
- Check the applicable state / institutional rules

Avoid making large payments based only on verbal promises.

---

# 17. Do FMGs receive a stipend during CRMI?

Stipend requirements can depend on the applicable regulations and notices.

The NMC has issued notices concerning compliance with stipend requirements for medical interns.

However, the exact amount, payment mechanism and institutional implementation should be verified from the current applicable rules and institution.

Do not assume that an amount reported by another FMG applies universally.

---

# 18. Can I work as a doctor after passing FMGE?

Passing FMGE alone should not be treated as equivalent to unrestricted permanent medical registration.

Whether and where an individual can practise depends on the registration status and applicable law.

An FMG should confirm the applicable registration status before undertaking independent medical practice.

---

# 19. Can I do NEET-PG after FMGE?

FMGE qualification and postgraduate admission are separate processes.

An FMG seeking postgraduate admission in India must satisfy the eligibility requirements applicable to the relevant postgraduate entrance examination and counselling process.

Passing FMGE should therefore not be interpreted as automatic admission eligibility for every postgraduate pathway.

---

# 20. Can I do residency / PG while completing CRMI?

Do not assume that CRMI and postgraduate training can simply be combined.

The candidate must satisfy the registration and eligibility requirements of the relevant postgraduate programme and the applicable internship requirements.

The rules of the relevant admission cycle and institution should be checked before making plans.

---

# 21. What documents should I keep after FMGE?

Keep a complete digital and physical record of:

- Passport
- Identity documents
- Foreign medical degree
- Academic transcripts
- Internship / clinical-training records
- FMGE application
- FMGE admit card
- FMGE result
- Pass certificate
- Provisional registration
- CRMI allotment letter
- Internship joining letter
- Attendance records
- Rotation records
- Internship completion certificate
- Registration correspondence
- Payment receipts
- Official emails and notices

Maintaining a chronological folder can make later registration much easier.

---

# 22. What if my name is different on my passport and degree?

Do not ignore the discrepancy.

The appropriate procedure depends on the nature of the difference and the requirements of the authority handling the application.

Possible supporting documentation may include:

- Affidavit
- Gazette documentation
- Name-change documentation
- Institutional clarification
- Other documents specified by the relevant authority

The candidate should verify the exact requirement rather than submitting an improvised solution.

---

# 23. What if my university or country is not on an internet "approved list"?

Do not rely on an unofficial list.

The absence of a university from a social-media list does not by itself establish ineligibility.

Likewise, appearing on an unofficial list does not establish eligibility.

Use the applicable NMC regulations and official guidance.

---

# 24. Can I trust Telegram or WhatsApp FMGE groups?

They can be useful for discovering practical issues.

They should not be treated as the final authority for regulatory requirements.

A useful approach is:

```text
Community report
      ↓
Identify the claimed rule/problem
      ↓
Find the official source
      ↓
Check whether it applies to your cohort/state
      ↓
Document the verified answer
```

This is one of the main purposes of the FMGE Open Guide.

---

# 25. What if an institution gives me instructions that conflict with NMC rules?

Ask the institution to provide the requirement in writing.

Then compare it with:

1. NMC regulations
2. NMC notices
3. State Government / Directorate of Medical Education instructions
4. State Medical Council instructions
5. Institutional written instructions

If the conflict remains unresolved, contact the relevant authority directly.

Do not rely solely on verbal statements.

# 28. How do I know whether information in this guide is reliable?

The guide uses a source hierarchy.

### Highest priority

- NMC regulations
- NMC official notices
- NBEMS Information Bulletins
- State Government / Directorate notices
- State Medical Council instructions

### Secondary

- Institution-issued written instructions

### Community evidence

- Dated FMG experiences
- Forum discussions
- Reddit / Telegram / WhatsApp reports

Community information is useful for identifying practical problems, but it is not automatically treated as a rule.

---

# 29. How can I report an error or outdated information?

If you find information that appears incorrect or outdated:

1. Check the cited source.
2. Check the date of the information.
3. Check whether a newer official notice exists.
4. Open an issue or submit a correction to the project.
5. Include the source supporting the correction.

When reporting an issue, provide:

- Page
- Section
- Incorrect statement
- Proposed correction
- Official source
- Date verified

---

# 30. What Should I Do If My Situation Is Complicated?

If your case involves unusual circumstances such as:

- Old admission cohort
- Transfer between universities
- Interrupted studies
- Multiple foreign institutions
- Internship completed abroad
- Name discrepancy
- Citizenship / OCI complications
- Previous registration
- Previous FMGE attempts
- Regulatory transition

do not rely on a generic FAQ answer.

Identify the exact regulation and obtain written clarification from the relevant authority.
