# FMGE Examination & Results

**Last verified:** 23 September 2026

This page explains the FMGE examination format, test-day process, passing standard, results and what happens after the result.

> **Important:** Examination procedures can change between sessions. The current NBEMS Information Bulletin and official notices for the relevant session take precedence over this guide.

---

# 1. FMGE Examination Structure

The FMGE consists of **one paper containing 300 multiple-choice questions**.

The examination is conducted in **two parts on the same day**.

| Part | Questions | Time |
|---|---:|---:|
| Part A | 150 | 150 minutes |
| Part B | 150 | 150 minutes |
| **Total** | **300** | **300 minutes** |

The examination is conducted as a computer-based test.

The questions are in English.

There is a scheduled break between the two parts.

---

# 2. Negative Marking

There is **no negative marking** in the FMGE.

A candidate's score is therefore based on the number of marks obtained from correctly answered questions, subject to the examination rules applicable to that session.

---

# 3. Passing Standard

A candidate is declared to have passed FMGE only after obtaining:

> **150 marks out of 300**

This corresponds to a **50% score**.

The pass standard is a fixed examination requirement; it is not a percentile-based qualifying cutoff.

---

# 4. Number of Attempts

The FMGE scheme does not impose a general limit on the number of attempts.

A candidate who does not qualify may apply for a subsequent session, subject to satisfying the eligibility and application requirements applicable to that session.

Each subsequent attempt requires following the current NBEMS application process.

---

# 5. Test-Day Process

The exact reporting and security procedures are specified in the session-specific Information Bulletin.

A typical sequence is:

```text
Report to examination centre
          ↓
Identity / document verification
          ↓
Security checks
          ↓
Biometric / registration procedures where applicable
          ↓
Computer allocation
          ↓
Part A
          ↓
Scheduled break
          ↓
Part B
          ↓
Completion of examination
```

Candidates should follow the instructions provided by NBEMS and the examination-centre staff.

---

# 6. Reporting Time

The reporting time is specified in the relevant Information Bulletin and admit card.

Candidates should distinguish between:

- Reporting time
- Entry closing time
- Examination start time

Arriving after the permitted entry time can result in denial of entry.

Do not use the reporting time from an older FMGE session.

---

# 7. What to Carry

The exact list of permitted documents and items is specified in the current Information Bulletin.

Candidates should check the bulletin and admit card before travelling to the examination centre.

Typically, candidates should be prepared with the required:

- Admit card
- Valid identity document
- Other documents specifically required by NBEMS

Only items expressly permitted by the examination instructions should be brought into the test area.

---

# 8. Electronic Devices

Electronic devices are generally prohibited inside the examination premises / test area according to NBEMS examination instructions.

This may include items such as:

- Mobile phones
- Smart watches
- Other electronic devices

Candidates should follow the current security instructions rather than relying on personal experience from previous examinations.

---

# 9. During the Examination

Candidates should follow the computer-based examination instructions displayed at the test centre.

Important principles include:

- Read each question carefully
- Select the intended response before moving on
- Follow the on-screen navigation instructions
- Do not attempt to access prohibited material
- Follow instructions from test-centre staff
- Do not engage in any activity prohibited by NBEMS

The detailed examination interface and procedures are determined by NBEMS.

---

# 10. Examination Irregularities

NBEMS may take action in cases involving:

- Unfair means
- Impersonation
- Prohibited electronic devices
- Misconduct
- Violation of examination instructions
- Other examination-security violations

Candidates should read the current bulletin's rules concerning unfair means and examination conduct.

---

# 11. Result

FMGE results are published by NBEMS.

The result is generally communicated as a **Pass / Fail** outcome for eligible candidates.

The official result should be obtained from the NBEMS website rather than from coaching platforms, social-media posts or third-party result lists.

---

# 12. Result Does Not Equal Registration

One of the most important distinctions for an FMG is:

> **Passing FMGE is not the same as obtaining permanent medical registration.**

A simplified pathway is:

```text
Pass FMGE
     ↓
Applicable provisional registration
     ↓
CRMI / internship where required
     ↓
Complete required documentation
     ↓
Apply for permanent registration
     ↓
Medical registration
```

The exact pathway depends on the regulations applicable to the individual candidate.

---

# 13. Pass Certificate

NBEMS issues / distributes FMGE pass certificates according to the procedure notified for the relevant session.

The procedure can change between sessions.

For example, NBEMS issued a specific notice regarding **in-person distribution of Pass Certificates for the FMGE June 2026 session**.

Candidates should therefore check the official notice following their examination result for:

- Whether a pass certificate is required
- Collection method
- Date and location
- Documents required
- Identity verification
- Any additional instructions

---

# 14. What Happens After Passing?

Passing FMGE is an important milestone, but an FMG may still have further regulatory steps.

A candidate should determine:

### Step 1 — Registration

Whether provisional registration with the relevant State Medical Council / authority is required.

### Step 2 — Internship

Whether CRMI must be completed in India.

### Step 3 — Completion documentation

Obtain the required internship completion and supporting documents.

### Step 4 — Permanent registration

Apply for permanent registration with the appropriate authority once all requirements have been fulfilled.

The applicable regulations and State Medical Council instructions should be checked for the individual's circumstances.

---

# 15. What Happens If You Fail?

A candidate who does not obtain the required passing score is not awarded a pass result for that attempt.

The candidate may prepare for a subsequent FMGE session, subject to the eligibility and application requirements applicable at that time.

There is no need to treat a failed attempt as a permanent bar on future attempts.

However, the candidate should check whether any outstanding eligibility or documentation issue needs to be resolved before reapplying.

---

# 16. Re-application After Failure

Before applying again:

- Check the next FMGE session
- Read the new Information Bulletin
- Confirm eligibility
- Check whether previously submitted documents remain valid / available
- Complete the new application
- Pay the applicable examination fee
- Follow the new session's instructions

Do not assume that a previous application automatically carries forward to the next session.

---

# 17. Re-evaluation and Rechecking

Candidates should not assume that an unsuccessful result can be changed through ordinary rechecking or re-evaluation.

The availability of any review, re-totaling or related process is determined by the applicable NBEMS rules and notices for that examination.

The current Information Bulletin should be consulted before making any request.

---

# 18. FMGE Score vs Rank

FMGE is a **qualifying examination**.

The primary outcome relevant to the licensing pathway is whether the candidate meets the required passing standard.

A candidate should not confuse:

- FMGE score
- FMGE pass/fail status
- NEET-PG rank
- Medical registration status

These are separate regulatory or examination outcomes.

---

# 19. FMGE and NEET-PG

Passing FMGE and qualifying for postgraduate medical education are separate matters.

A foreign medical graduate seeking postgraduate admission in India must satisfy the requirements applicable to the relevant postgraduate entrance examination and counselling process.

FMGE qualification should therefore not be interpreted as an automatic postgraduate admission qualification.

---

# 20. Common Misconceptions

### "I passed FMGE, so I am automatically registered."

**Incorrect.**

Further registration and internship requirements may remain.

### "50% means I need a percentile."

**Incorrect.**

The FMGE passing standard is expressed as a minimum number of marks out of 300.

### "There is negative marking, so I should leave uncertain questions blank."

**Do not assume this.**

FMGE has no negative marking under the examination scheme described by NBEMS.

### "If I fail once, I cannot write FMGE again."

**Incorrect.**

The examination scheme does not impose a general attempt limit.

### "My FMGE result automatically gives me a medical licence."

**Incorrect.**

Registration is a separate regulatory process.

### "Old FMGE rules will apply to my next attempt."

**Not necessarily.**

Always check the bulletin for the specific session.
