# FMGE Eligibility

**Last verified:** 23 September 2026

This page explains the general eligibility framework for the Foreign Medical Graduate Examination (FMGE).

> **Important:** Eligibility requirements are determined by the applicable regulations and the current examination bulletin issued by the National Board of Examinations in Medical Sciences (NBEMS). Always check the current bulletin before applying.

---

# 1. What is FMGE?

The **Foreign Medical Graduate Examination (FMGE)** is the screening examination conducted by the National Board of Examinations in Medical Sciences (NBEMS) for eligible Indian citizens and certain Overseas Citizens of India (OCIs) who hold primary medical qualifications awarded by medical institutions outside India.

The examination is part of the regulatory pathway for obtaining medical registration in India where the applicable rules require it.

---

# 2. Who May Need FMGE?

A person who obtained their primary medical qualification from a medical institution outside India may need to qualify through FMGE before obtaining medical registration in India, subject to the regulations applicable to that individual.

The requirement should be determined using:

- The date of admission to the foreign medical course
- The country and institution where the qualification was obtained
- The nature of the primary medical qualification
- Citizenship / OCI status
- The applicable NMC regulations
- Any exemptions or transitional provisions

---

# 3. Basic Eligibility Framework

Before applying, an applicant should verify the following:

### Medical qualification

The applicant must possess, or be eligible to possess by the relevant cut-off date, a primary medical qualification from an institution outside India that satisfies the applicable regulatory requirements.

### Recognition of qualification

The qualification must satisfy the requirements applicable to foreign medical graduates.

> NMC does not endorse a general list of foreign medical universities or institutions. Applicants should therefore assess their qualification against the applicable regulations rather than relying solely on whether a university appears on an informal "NMC-approved" list.

### Citizenship / OCI status

FMGE eligibility depends on the applicant's citizenship or OCI status as specified in the applicable regulations and examination bulletin.

### Documentary evidence

The applicant must be able to provide the documents required by NBEMS to establish eligibility.

---

# 4. Foreign Medical Qualification

Possession of a foreign medical degree by itself should not be treated as an automatic guarantee of eligibility for medical registration in India.

The applicable regulatory framework may consider factors such as:

- Course duration
- Clinical training
- Internship
- Medium and location of training
- Recognition of the qualification in the country where it was awarded
- Eligibility for registration in the country of qualification
- Other conditions specified by NMC

The relevant NMC regulations should be consulted for the applicant's admission cohort.

---

# 5. Admission Date Matters

One of the most important details for an FMG is:

> **When did you begin your foreign medical course?**

Different regulatory frameworks and transitional provisions may apply to different admission cohorts.

An applicant should therefore retain documentation showing:

- Date of admission
- Name of institution
- Course entered
- Course duration
- Academic records
- Degree / qualification awarded

Do not determine eligibility solely from information provided by agents, consultants, social-media groups or other applicants.

---

# 6. FMGE vs Medical Registration

Passing FMGE and obtaining medical registration are **not the same event**.

A simplified pathway may look like:

```text
Foreign medical qualification
          ↓
FMGE / applicable licensing requirement
          ↓
Provisional registration
          ↓
CRMI / internship where required
          ↓
Completion documentation
          ↓
Permanent medical registration
```

The exact pathway depends on the regulations applicable to the individual.

---

# 7. Documents

The exact document list can change between examination sessions.

Depending on the applicable bulletin, applicants may be required to provide documentation relating to:

- Identity
- Citizenship / OCI status
- Passport
- Primary medical qualification
- Academic records
- Internship / clinical training
- Eligibility documents
- Name or date-of-birth discrepancies
- Other documents specified by NBEMS

**Always use the current FMGE Information Bulletin as the controlling document.**

---

# 8. Common Eligibility Mistakes

### Mistake 1 — Assuming the university is "NMC approved"

NMC's official information for students studying abroad does not provide a simple universal endorsement list of foreign medical universities.

Instead, applicants should assess their qualification against the applicable regulatory requirements.

### Mistake 2 — Ignoring the admission year

The regulations applicable to an FMG can depend on the date of admission.

### Mistake 3 — Confusing FMGE eligibility with registration eligibility

Being eligible to sit for FMGE does not by itself establish that all requirements for permanent medical registration have been fulfilled.

### Mistake 4 — Relying on old Telegram / WhatsApp information

FMGE requirements and documentation can change.

Always compare community advice with the latest:

1. NMC regulations
2. NMC notices
3. NBEMS FMGE bulletin
4. State Medical Council instructions

# 10. Source Hierarchy

When two sources provide conflicting information, verify in approximately this order:

1. **Current NMC regulation**
2. **Current NMC official notice**
3. **Current NBEMS FMGE Information Bulletin**
4. **State Medical Council / Government instructions**
5. **Institutional written instructions**
6. **Documented community experience**
7. **Social-media discussion**

Community reports can identify issues, but should not automatically be treated as regulatory requirements.

---

# 11. Applicant Checklist

Before applying for FMGE, confirm:

- [ ] My foreign medical qualification falls within the applicable regulatory framework
- [ ] I have verified my admission date
- [ ] I have verified my citizenship / OCI eligibility
- [ ] I have the required identity documents
- [ ] I have the required qualification documents
- [ ] I have checked the current NBEMS FMGE bulletin
- [ ] I understand that passing FMGE does not itself complete medical registration
- [ ] I have checked whether any additional NMC requirements apply to my admission cohort
