# Compulsory Rotating Medical Internship (CRMI) for FMGs — 2026–27

**Last verified:** 23 September 2026

**Information period:** Academic Year 2026–27

> This page records information published by the National Medical Commission (NMC) concerning allocation of Compulsory Rotating Medical Internship (CRMI) slots to Foreign Medical Graduates (FMGs). It should not be treated as a substitute for the latest instructions issued by the NMC, State Medical Council, Directorate of Medical Education, or individual institution.

---

## NMC allocation of CRMI slots for FMGs

On **10 March 2026**, the National Medical Commission (NMC), through the Under-Graduate Medical Education Board (UGMEB), issued a circular concerning allocation of CRMI slots for Foreign Medical Graduates.

The circular addresses the issue of non-allotment or shortage of internship slots for FMGs and directs State Medical Councils to undertake allocation of available slots in coordination with the respective Directorates of Medical Education.

### Allocation criteria

According to the NMC circular, the following criteria were indicated for allocation of CRMI slots to FMGs:

- **7.5%** of the permitted intake of interns in established medical colleges/institutions
- **100%** of CRMI seats for FMGs in new medical colleges
- **100%** of CRMI seats corresponding to enhanced seats in existing medical colleges where seats were increased between academic years **2022–23 and 2025–26**

### Total slots identified

NMC stated that a total of:

**43,250 CRMI internship slots**

would be available to FMGs for **Academic Year 2026–27**, in addition to seats assigned in listed and erstwhile MCI-approved non-teaching hospitals/institutions conducting CRMI programmes.

The NMC circular includes state/UT-wise and institution-wise matrices of the identified internship slots.

---

## Who is responsible for allotment?

The NMC circular directs **State Medical Councils** to initiate and undertake the allotment process for available internship slots.

This is to be done:

- In coordination with the relevant Directorate of Medical Education
- For eligible Foreign Medical Graduates
- In accordance with applicable rules, regulations, public notices, and circulars

Therefore, the existence of a listed CRMI slot should not automatically be interpreted as a guarantee that an individual FMG can directly join that institution without completing the applicable state-level process.

---

## Important distinction: "available slot" vs "individual allotment"

This distinction is important.

The NMC circular provides a framework and identifies available internship capacity.

It does **not** mean that every FMG can independently approach any listed institution and claim a particular slot.

The actual process may involve:

1. Eligibility
2. State Medical Council requirements
3. Directorate of Medical Education processes
4. Institutional requirements
5. Allotment
6. Registration/document verification
7. Joining formalities

The applicable procedure should therefore be checked with the relevant authorities for the candidate's state and circumstances.
