# FMGE Study Guide

A structured, evidence-aware study guide for the Foreign Medical Graduate Examination (FMGE).

This section focuses on **how to prepare for FMGE**, rather than the administrative process of applying for or registering for the examination.

## Start Here

- [FMGE Study Blueprint](blueprint.md)
- [FMGE Study Guide](study-guide.md)

## Preparation Framework

The guide is organized around:

1. Understanding the examination
2. Building a subject-wise foundation
3. Solving and reviewing MCQs
4. Identifying weak areas
5. Spaced repetition and revision
6. Mock examinations
7. Final revision

## Evidence and Blueprint

Not all preparation information has the same evidentiary status.

The guide distinguishes between:

- Official examination information
- Published examination regulations and bulletins
- Documented question patterns
- Historical examination observations
- Community-reported preparation strategies
- Editorial recommendations

A preparation recommendation should not be presented as an official NBEMS requirement unless it is supported by an official source.

## Resources

Resources may include:

- Textbooks
- Question banks
- Previous-question resources
- Video courses
- Flashcards
- Revision notes
- Mock examinations
- Free educational resources

Commercial resources are listed as resources, not endorsements.

## Subject Guides

Subject-specific preparation guides will be added progressively.

Each guide should aim to cover:

- What to study
- What to prioritize
- Commonly tested areas
- MCQ strategy
- Revision strategy
- High-yield concepts
- Common mistakes
- Recommended resources

## Important

FMGE preparation strategies vary between candidates.

The purpose of this guide is to provide a structured framework and evidence-based information, not to prescribe one universal study method.
