# Medical Registration — Foreign Medical Graduates

**Last verified:** 23 September 2026

This page explains the general medical-registration pathway for Foreign Medical Graduates (FMGs) after completing the applicable examination and internship requirements.

> **Important:** Registration requirements can depend on the individual's circumstances and the regulations applicable to them. Always verify current requirements with the National Medical Commission (NMC) and the relevant State Medical Council.

---

# 1. Registration Authorities

Medical registration in India involves both national and state-level regulatory structures.

### National Medical Commission

The **National Medical Commission (NMC)** establishes the national regulatory framework for medical education and registration.

Official website:

https://www.nmc.org.in/

### State Medical Councils

State Medical Councils handle registration-related processes within their respective jurisdictions.

An FMG should identify the State Medical Council responsible for their registration and follow its current instructions.

---

# 2. General FMG Registration Pathway

For an FMG who is required to complete the Indian internship pathway, the process can generally be represented as:

```text
Foreign medical qualification
        ↓
Applicable screening / licensing examination
        ↓
Provisional registration
        ↓
Compulsory Rotating Medical Internship (CRMI)
        ↓
Internship completion documentation
        ↓
Application for permanent registration
        ↓
Permanent medical registration
```

This is a simplified representation.

The exact sequence and documentation can vary depending on the applicable regulations and the individual's circumstances.

---

# 3. Provisional Registration

Provisional registration is associated with the stage in which an eligible FMG is permitted to undertake the required internship/CRMI.

The FMG should verify:

- Which authority issues the provisional registration
- Whether provisional registration has been granted before joining
- The validity period
- Conditions attached to the registration
- Documents required
- Whether the registration covers the specific internship institution

An FMG should not assume that passing the licensing/screening examination automatically creates permission to begin internship.

---

# 4. Compulsory Rotating Medical Internship

Applicable FMGs may be required to complete CRMI in India before obtaining permanent registration.

The National Medical Commission publishes regulations and notices concerning CRMI.

For AY 2026–27, NMC published an institution-wise framework identifying CRMI seats available to FMGs.

The internship allocation process may additionally involve:

- State Medical Councils
- Directorate of Medical Education
- Medical colleges
- Teaching hospitals
- Other competent authorities

See the state-specific pages in this guide for documented local procedures.

---

# 5. Permanent Registration

After completing the applicable internship requirements, an FMG can proceed with the permanent-registration process under the regulations applicable to them.

The applicant may need to provide evidence including:

- Primary medical qualification
- Examination qualification
- Provisional registration
- CRMI completion
- Identity documents
- Photographs
- Other documents specified by the registering authority

The exact document list should be obtained from the relevant State Medical Council.

---

# 6. State Medical Council

The State Medical Council is particularly important because registration procedures can include state-specific administrative requirements.

Before applying, check the council's current official instructions for:

- Application method
- Required documents
- Fees
- Verification
- Appointment requirements
- Original-document requirements
- Processing time
- Registration certificate
- Renewal or other post-registration requirements

Do not rely on an old checklist or another applicant's experience as proof of the current requirements.

---

# 7. Documents

A registration file may contain documents such as:

- Passport
- Identity document
- Primary medical qualification
- Academic transcripts
- FMGE/FMGL examination documentation
- Clerkship documentation
- Provisional registration
- CRMI joining documentation
- CRMI completion certificate
- Internship attendance records
- Passport photographs
- Affidavits or declarations where applicable
- Other documents requested by the relevant authority

The exact list is authority- and case-dependent.

---

# 8. Verification

Some registration processes involve verification of educational and identity documents.

An applicant should:

1. Check the current official checklist.
2. Confirm whether originals are required.
3. Confirm whether notarisation, attestation or verification is required.
4. Keep scanned copies of every submitted document.
5. Keep receipts and acknowledgement numbers.
6. Record the date and method of submission.

---

# 9. Common Registration Problems

FMGs may encounter administrative problems such as:

- Missing documentation
- Differences between old and current requirements
- Delays in verification
- Delays in provisional registration
- Internship completion records not being updated
- Problems obtaining certificates
- Communication difficulties
- Unclear responsibility between institutions and authorities

When documenting such problems in this guide, distinguish between:

**Official requirement** → supported by an authority

**Institutional requirement** → applies to a particular institution

**Individual experience** → reported by an applicant

**Interpretation** → explanation of a published rule

---

# 10. Before Submitting an Application

A useful preparation checklist is:

- [ ] Identify the correct State Medical Council
- [ ] Read the current official registration instructions
- [ ] Download the current application/checklist
- [ ] Prepare original documents if required
- [ ] Prepare scanned copies
- [ ] Confirm photographs and format requirements
- [ ] Confirm applicable fees
- [ ] Confirm whether an appointment is required
- [ ] Record the application/acknowledgement number
- [ ] Keep proof of payment
- [ ] Keep copies of everything submitted

---

# 11. Source Hierarchy

For registration questions, use sources in this order:

1. **Current NMC regulations and official notices**
2. **Current State Medical Council instructions**
3. **Directorate of Medical Education / State Government notices**
4. **Institutional instructions**
5. **Community reports and applicant experiences**

A community post can identify a problem worth investigating, but should not automatically be treated as an official requirement.
