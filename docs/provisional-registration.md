# Provisional Medical Registration for FMGs

**Last verified:** 23 September 2026

This page explains the general framework for provisional medical registration of Foreign Medical Graduates (FMGs) in India.

> **Important:** The exact application process, documents, fees and authority may vary according to the applicable regulations and the State Medical Council. Always verify the current instructions before applying.

---

# 1. What Is Provisional Registration?

Provisional registration is the registration stage that may allow an eligible medical graduate to undertake the required supervised internship / CRMI before obtaining permanent medical registration.

For an FMG, a simplified pathway may be:

```text
Foreign medical qualification
          ↓
FMGE / applicable licensing requirement
          ↓
Provisional registration
          ↓
CRMI / internship
          ↓
Internship completion
          ↓
Permanent registration
```

The exact pathway depends on the regulations applicable to the individual.

---

# 2. Is Provisional Registration the Same as Permanent Registration?

**No.**

They serve different purposes.

### Provisional registration

Generally relates to the period during which an eligible graduate undertakes the required supervised internship / training.

### Permanent registration

Is the registration stage applicable after the candidate has fulfilled the required conditions for full medical registration.

An FMG should not assume that obtaining provisional registration means they can practise independently without restrictions.

---

# 3. Who May Need Provisional Registration?

An FMG who is required to complete CRMI in India may need provisional registration before starting the internship.

The applicable requirements can depend on:

- FMG regulations
- Date of admission
- Foreign medical qualification
- FMGE status
- Citizenship / OCI status
- Previous registration
- Internship requirements
- State Medical Council procedure

Individual cases should be checked against the applicable regulations.

---

# 4. Which Authority Handles Provisional Registration?

Medical registration is administered through the applicable medical-registration authority.

For many FMGs, the relevant process involves a **State Medical Council**.

The National Medical Commission provides the national regulatory framework, while State Medical Councils handle applicable registration functions within their jurisdiction.

Therefore:

> Do not assume that an FMG can simply apply to any State Medical Council of their choice.

The appropriate authority depends on the applicable rules and the candidate's circumstances.

---

# 5. When Should Provisional Registration Be Obtained?

An FMG should determine the provisional-registration requirement **before beginning CRMI**.

A practical sequence is:

```text
FMGE result
     ↓
Pass certificate / applicable documentation
     ↓
Provisional registration application
     ↓
Registration granted
     ↓
CRMI allotment / joining
     ↓
Internship
```

The exact order can differ depending on the state and current administrative procedure.

Candidates should not begin clinical internship assuming that registration can be completed retrospectively.

---

# 6. Typical Documents

The exact document list is determined by the relevant authority.

Depending on the case, an applicant may need documents such as:

### Identity

- Passport
- Government identity document
- Passport photographs
- Citizenship / OCI documentation where applicable

### Medical qualification

- Foreign medical degree
- Provisional degree, where applicable
- Academic transcripts
- Other qualification documents

### FMGE

- FMGE result
- FMGE pass certificate
- Other NBEMS documentation where required

### Registration / internship

- Provisional registration application
- Internship / CRMI allotment documentation where applicable
- Other documents requested by the State Medical Council

### Supporting documents

Depending on the case:

- Affidavit
- Name-change documentation
- Address proof
- Verification documents
- Additional certificates

The current State Medical Council checklist should always be treated as controlling.

---

# 7. Document Verification

Registration authorities may require verification of documents.

This can involve:

- Identity verification
- Qualification verification
- FMGE verification
- Foreign university documentation
- Passport verification
- Other regulatory checks

Applicants should ensure that names, dates and other identifying information are consistent across documents.

A discrepancy should be resolved rather than ignored.

---

# 8. Name and Date-of-Birth Discrepancies

Common examples include:

- Different spelling of name
- Missing middle name
- Different surname format
- Different date-of-birth format
- Passport and degree showing different details

Do not assume that a minor discrepancy is harmless.

The appropriate correction or supporting documentation depends on the authority and the nature of the discrepancy.

If clarification is required, obtain it from the registration authority in writing.

---

# 9. Application Workflow

The exact workflow differs by State Medical Council, but a general model is:

```text
Check eligibility
       ↓
Identify correct State Medical Council
       ↓
Obtain current application instructions
       ↓
Prepare documents
       ↓
Submit application
       ↓
Pay applicable fee
       ↓
Document verification
       ↓
Deficiency / clarification if required
       ↓
Provisional registration issued
```

Some authorities may use online applications, while others may require additional physical verification or document submission.

---

# 10. Fees

Registration fees are determined by the relevant authority.

Do not rely on:

- Old fee screenshots
- Telegram messages
- WhatsApp forwards
- Fees quoted by agents
- Fees charged by unrelated institutions

Before payment:

- Check the official fee schedule
- Confirm the purpose of the fee
- Save the payment receipt
- Keep the application number

If an institution or intermediary asks for an additional payment, request an official written explanation and receipt.

---

# 11. Processing Delays

Applications may be delayed because of:

- Missing documents
- Incorrect documents
- Verification requirements
- Name discrepancies
- Incomplete application
- Payment problems
- High application volume
- Additional clarification requests
- Administrative delays

A delay does not necessarily mean that an application has been rejected.

Applicants should first check whether the authority has identified a specific deficiency.

---

# 12. Deficiency in an Application

If the registration authority requests additional information or documents:

1. Read the request carefully.
2. Identify exactly what is missing.
3. Obtain the required document.
4. Submit it using the prescribed method.
5. Keep proof of submission.
6. Follow up using the official contact channel if necessary.

Do not repeatedly submit different documents without understanding what deficiency has been raised.

---

# 13. Provisional Registration and CRMI

Provisional registration is closely connected to the internship stage for FMGs who are required to complete CRMI.

A simplified relationship is:

```text
FMGE qualification
       ↓
Provisional registration
       ↓
CRMI
       ↓
Completion certificate
       ↓
Permanent registration
```

However, the actual administrative sequence can vary by state.

The CRMI page should therefore always be read together with the relevant State Medical Council instructions.

See:

- [FMG Internship & CRMI](internship/index.md)
- [State-by-State FMG Guide](states/index.md)

---

# 14. What Does Provisional Registration Permit?

Provisional registration should be understood within the purpose for which it is issued.

For an FMG completing required internship, it generally relates to the supervised internship / training stage rather than unrestricted independent medical practice.

An FMG should check the wording and conditions of the registration issued by the relevant authority.

Do not assume that:

> **Provisional registration = unrestricted medical licence**

They are not equivalent.

---

# 15. Can an FMG Start CRMI Without Provisional Registration?

Do not assume that this is permitted.

Whether provisional registration must be obtained before joining CRMI depends on the applicable regulatory and state procedure.

Before joining, verify:

- Registration requirement
- Registration status
- Allotment letter
- Institution's joining requirements
- State Medical Council instructions

Starting clinical work without the required registration can create regulatory and documentation problems.

---

# 16. What If the State Medical Council Has Not Processed the Application?

First determine the status of the application.

Check:

- Application number
- Online status, if available
- Deficiency notices
- Official email
- Official telephone / helpdesk
- Physical verification requirements

When contacting the authority, provide:

- Full name
- Application number
- Date of application
- Type of registration
- Relevant supporting information

Avoid sending sensitive personal documents to unofficial individuals or groups unless there is a legitimate reason and secure channel.

---

# 17. Registration and State Choice

An FMG should not choose a State Medical Council solely because another applicant reported faster processing there.

The appropriate registration authority depends on the applicable regulatory framework and the candidate's circumstances.

Anecdotal reports of faster or easier processing should be treated as community experience, not as a regulatory rule.

---

# 18. Permanent Registration After CRMI

After completing the required internship, an FMG may become eligible to apply for permanent registration, subject to satisfying the applicable requirements.

A simplified pathway is:

```text
Provisional registration
        ↓
CRMI
        ↓
Internship completion certificate
        ↓
Required supporting documents
        ↓
Permanent registration application
        ↓
Verification
        ↓
Permanent registration
```

The permanent-registration application should be treated as a separate process.

Completing CRMI does not automatically mean that permanent registration has already been granted.

# 20. Common Mistakes

### Mistake 1 — Applying to the wrong authority

Identify the correct State Medical Council before submitting an application.

### Mistake 2 — Using an old document checklist

Registration requirements can change.

### Mistake 3 — Starting CRMI without confirming registration requirements

Verify the status required before joining.

### Mistake 4 — Ignoring deficiencies

A missing document can delay processing.

### Mistake 5 — Paying unofficial fees

Request official fee information and receipts.

### Mistake 6 — Losing application records

Keep application numbers, receipts and correspondence.

### Mistake 7 — Assuming provisional registration permits independent practice

Understand the scope and conditions of the registration issued.
